🛡️ User Protection Policy

72-Hour Guarantee & Refund Policy Statement

At VELORA, we strive for instant account activation upon payment verification. However, to protect your funds and ensure complete transparency, we operate under a strict 72-Hour Fulfillment Guarantee.

72-Hour Resolution Window

If your VELORA account setup or activation is not completed within 72 hours of successful payment confirmation, you are eligible for an immediate, full refund.

Automatic Refund Initiation

Once a 72-hour delay is verified, the paid amount will be refunded directly back to the original bank account or card used during payment via our payment processor (Squad / GTBank).

Processing Timelines

While refunds are processed instantly on our end upon approval, local banking networks may take 1 to 3 business days to reflect the funds in your bank account statement.

Exceptions

Refunds will not apply if the delay is caused by inaccurate registration details provided by the user (e.g., invalid phone numbers, wrong email addresses, or unverified WhatsApp contact).
